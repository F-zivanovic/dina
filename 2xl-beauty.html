// The Challenge

2XL Beauty started with an ambitious idea: create a beauty salon where clients could find everything in one place - from hair and nail services to beauty treatments and additional services. The goal was not simply to open another salon, but to build a recognizable brand from day one. The owner wanted 2XL Beauty to be visible everywhere and was ready to invest in strong branding and marketing to quickly build awareness, attract new clients, and generate bookings.

// The Process

We started from the ground up by creating the complete 2XL Beauty brand identity - logo, visual direction, and all supporting materials. This expanded across the entire physical space and customer experience, including uniforms, hoodies, cups, mugs, mirrors, wall graphics, and both interior and exterior salon branding. From there, we designed and developed the website and created the service and pricing materials. At the same time, we launched a complete digital strategy across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and Google, ensuring the brand was consistent and recognizable across every touchpoint.

// The Outcome

Two years after opening, our partnership with 2XL Beauty continues to grow. Across social media, the brand has generated 2M+ in total reach, while a consistent content and marketing strategy has helped build strong demand, with appointments regularly booked up to a week in advance. 2XL Beauty has also built a strong reputation in Belgrade, particularly for hair extensions, while collaborations with influencers have helped expand its visibility even further. Today, we continue managing and improving the brand's digital presence across social media and Google, with Google Ads becoming the latest step in expanding its reach and attracting new clients.
