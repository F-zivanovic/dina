Lumen

Lumen is an online psychology counseling practice offering individual and couples sessions. We designed their visual identity and website to reflect warmth, professionalism, and trust.

Client

Collaboration Since

Duration

Logo Design Website UI/UX & Development Brand Identity Visual Identity System

// The Challenge

Two psychologists came to us with the idea of building their own counseling practice from the ground up. They needed a professional identity and an online presence that would communicate trust, warmth, and credibility, while also helping potential clients easily learn more about their services and get in touch.

// The Process

We started by creating Lumen’s logo and complete brand identity, establishing a visual direction that felt professional, calm, and approachable. Once the brand was defined, we designed and developed their website around a simple and intuitive user experience. We also implemented an inquiry system that allows potential clients to contact Lumen directly through the website, with every inquiry delivered straight to their email.

// The Outcome

Lumen launched successfully with a complete visual identity and website that immediately attracted its first clients. The site enables effortless online session booking, while the brand clearly communicates the practice's professionalism and empathy.
