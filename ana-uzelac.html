ANA By Ana Uzelac

ANA By Ana Uzelac is an exclusive high-end brand specializing in pure silk scarves, blending timeless artistic expressions with modern textile aesthetics.

// The Challenge

Ana had been working with silk for years, creating original prints and handcrafted scarves made from 100% silk. While the product and craftsmanship were already there, sales relied mostly on word of mouth, which limited the brand’s reach. The goal was to take ANA by Ana Uzelac to the next level by creating an online store where customers could easily discover and purchase the products, while also building a stronger presence on Google.

// The Process

Since Ana already had an established logo she wanted to keep, we focused directly on creating the digital experience around the existing brand. We designed and developed a complete e-commerce website, structured the product collections, created a smooth shopping experience, and integrated Stripe for secure online payments. The store was built with everything needed to present and sell the silk collections professionally online.

// The Outcome

ANA by Ana Uzelac now has a complete e-commerce platform that gives the brand a professional online presence and a foundation for future growth. Since June 2026, we have also been working on the brand’s social media and online visibility, with the goal of bringing more potential customers to the website and increasing direct sales over time. The project is still in its early growth stage - but with the right foundation in place, the focus is now on building consistent visibility, traffic, and long-term results.
