KGB e-sports

KGB e-sports is a legendary gaming center in Belgrade, Serbia, operating since 2003. We manage their social media presence across Instagram and TikTok, handle sponsor communications, and designed their corporate memorandum.

// The Challenge

KGB e-sports is one of the Balkans' most established gaming centers with over 20 years of history. The challenge was translating their legendary in-person energy into a compelling social media presence that resonates with a new generation of gamers, while also building professional sponsor relationships through targeted outreach.

// The Process

We took over their Instagram and TikTok channels, developing a content strategy that showcases the gaming center's atmosphere, competitive events, and community culture. On the sponsor side, we crafted professional email campaigns and outreach materials to connect KGB with hardware brands and esports organizations.

// The Outcome

The revamped social media presence rapidly grew KGB's online following, bringing in a younger audience and boosting foot traffic during events. Our sponsor outreach secured partnerships with key gaming industry brands, strengthening KGB's position as a premier esports venue in the region.
