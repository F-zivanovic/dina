PawsToTail

PawsToTail is a US-based online veterinary service making pet healthcare accessible from anywhere.

// The Challenge

Paws2Tail needed a digital presence that makes online veterinary consultations feel trustworthy and easy. The challenge was designing a seamless booking experience, alongside educational booklets and flyers that pet owners would actually engage with - all while maintaining a friendly, professional brand voice.

// The Process

We built a clean, pet-friendly website with an integrated pop-up booking system for scheduling online consultations. Alongside the web experience, we designed a series of digital booklets covering pet health topics and eye-catching flyers for promotional outreach - all tied together with a cohesive, approachable visual language.

// The Outcome

Paws2Tail launched with a polished digital ecosystem that converted curious pet owners into booked consultations. The pop-up booking flow reduced scheduling friction, the booklets drove organic engagement on social media, and the flyer campaigns successfully expanded their reach into new communities.
