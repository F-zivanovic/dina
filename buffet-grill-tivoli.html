Buffet Grill Tivoli

Buffet Grill Tivoli is a beloved seaside restaurant in Crikvenica, Croatia, known for its grilled specialties and Mediterranean cuisine. We designed their complete brand identity, website, and menu.

// The Challenge

Buffet Grill Tivoli needed a fresh visual identity that captures the warm, Mediterranean spirit of their seaside location in Crikvenica. The goal was to create a brand that feels welcoming to both locals and tourists, with a modern website for easy discovery and a beautifully designed menu.

// The Process

We crafted a brand identity rooted in warm, earthy tones with Mediterranean accents - evoking sun-kissed coastlines and open-fire grilling. The logo blends rustic charm with modern simplicity. The website showcases the restaurant's atmosphere and location, while the printed menu was designed to feel premium yet approachable.

// The Outcome

Buffet Grill Tivoli relaunched with a cohesive brand presence that immediately elevated its visibility among tourists visiting Crikvenica. The new website drives foot traffic through clear location info and appetizing photography, while the redesigned menu has become a signature part of the dining experience.
